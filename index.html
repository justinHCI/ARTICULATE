The project

Science should not need a translator.

ARTICULATE brings together machine translation, conversational AI and speech technology to make scientific knowledge accessible beyond academia and English-speaking audiences.

We translate not only across languages, but across styles: from the formal language of science into everyday, engaging dialogue that learners can listen to, question and shape.

Research approach

A dialogue to think with.

ARTICULATE supports self-regulated learning through a pair of role-based conversational agents, grounded in translated scientific documents and responsive to each learner's language profile.

01 / Expert

Scientific depth

Explains concepts with accuracy and context, using the learner's target language.

02 / Peer

Everyday voice

Asks the questions that make difficult ideas approachable, using dialect and informal style.

03 / Learner

Active participation

Listens, interjects and co-creates understanding through questions, remarks and ideas.

Objectives

Four commitments.

  1. 01High-quality, document-level translation of scientific material into low-resource and dialectal languages.
  2. 02Expert and Peer AI agents that create authentic, co-constructive science dialogue.
  3. 03Robust safeguards that maintain factual accuracy and prevent misinformation and harmful content.
  4. 04Speech-capable avatars integrating automatic speech recognition and text-to-speech in the target dialect.
